About the BBB Program

Believe, Belong and Blossom (BBB) is a six-week leadership program designed for girls from culturally diverse backgrounds.

Through engaging activities, meaningful conversations and shared experiences, participants are encouraged to explore who they are, develop confidence in themselves and strengthen their sense of belonging.

Each session runs for approximately 90 minutes, providing a supportive environment where young women can connect, learn from one another and consider the possibilities available to them.

What the Program Explores

Six key areas of exploration and development.

Identity

Exploring personal identity, culture, values and the experiences that shape us.

Gender Assumptions

Challenging gender assumptions and stereotypes and considering their impact.

Intercultural Understanding

Developing understanding, respect and communication across cultures.

Belonging

Building connections and strengthening feelings of inclusion and belonging.

Thought Work

Reflecting on perspectives, assumptions, values and the way we think about others.

Planning for the Future

Exploring future opportunities, career possibilities and personal aspirations.

The Aims of BBB

Confidence and Connection

To support nominated students to develop intercultural understanding and communication skills, increase self-awareness and confidence, and strengthen their connections with other young women in the group and beyond.

Learning Through Experience

To provide experiential learning through games, discussions, storytelling, skill development and reflection activities exploring culture, communication, prejudice, justice, identities and values.

Future Opportunities

To increase students' awareness of future career opportunities and possibilities, encouraging them to think positively about their potential and aspirations.

A Stronger Sense of Belonging

To create opportunities for young women to share their experiences, learn from different perspectives and recognise the value of their own identities and those of others.
