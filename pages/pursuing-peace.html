About the Program

Learning to live together peacefully

The Abraham Institute's School Presentations, which are based on the Together for Humanity model, aim to assist with the development of children's Intercultural Understanding in South Australian schools.

Hatred and intolerance, or acceptance and respect, are learnt early. Prejudice and hatred dividing people who come from different cultural and religious backgrounds are a threat to a harmonious society in Australia.

The Abraham Institute works in South Australian schools to help young people learn to co-exist peacefully through intercultural understanding.

We'd love to come to your school and work with your students, exploring the ideas of belonging, values, respect, harmony and peace.

The P2 Experience

Creating opportunities for connection

Through activities, games, small-group discussions and questions and answers, students are encouraged to explore their own beliefs about themselves and each other.

The P2 program creates a safe space for students to explore their ideas, ask questions and develop greater intercultural awareness.

Students have the opportunity to meet educators from diverse religious and cultural backgrounds.

What Students Learn

Understanding what connects us

Through P2, students learn through interaction and question and answer that there are many ways to be Australian, and that it is our shared values that make it so.

Students explore how important it is for each one of us to feel included and like we are part of society.

The program affords students the opportunity to meet educators from diverse religious and cultural backgrounds, perhaps for the first time, and provides a safe space for them to ask questions about different cultures and religions.

Through P2, students explore:

  • Belonging and inclusion
  • Personal and shared values
  • Cultural and religious diversity
  • Respect and understanding
  • Identity and assumptions
  • Harmony and peaceful coexistence
