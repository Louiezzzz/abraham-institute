Intercultural Understanding Partnerships

Supporting schools to strengthen intercultural understanding, address community challenges and improve student learning and wellbeing.

Building Stronger, More Inclusive School Communities

The Intercultural Understanding Partnership (ICUP) is a national initiative created by Together For Humanity (TFH), in partnership with the Jewish Christian Muslim Association (JCMA) in Victoria and The Abraham Institute in South Australia.

The initiative works with schools to identify and respond to cross-cultural and interfaith challenges within their communities. Through consultation with intercultural experts and financial support, participating schools can develop approaches that strengthen student learning, connection and wellbeing.

Recognising that every school community is different, the partnership supports schools to identify challenges relevant to their own circumstances and work towards meaningful, sustainable responses.

How Schools Are Supported

Practical support to help schools address intercultural and interfaith challenges within their communities.

Expert Consultation

Schools receive consultation time with intercultural experts to explore their particular challenges, consider different perspectives and identify possible approaches.

Financial Support

Successful schools receive financial support to help implement initiatives and practical responses that address their identified community challenge.

Stronger School Communities

The partnership helps schools work towards more inclusive and connected communities where students can learn from and engage with people from different backgrounds.

Student Learning and Wellbeing

By addressing intercultural and interfaith challenges, schools can work towards improving the learning experiences and wellbeing of their students.
