WHO WE ARE

About The Abraham Institute

Promoting interfaith and intercultural dialogue, understanding and respect.

OUR PURPOSE

Building understanding. Strengthening communities.

The Abraham Institute is an educational organisation that promotes interfaith and intercultural dialogue and understanding.

We work with people from diverse religious and cultural backgrounds to encourage meaningful conversations, develop mutual respect and build stronger connections.

Through education, school programs and community engagement, we aim to create a more peaceful, accepting and inclusive society.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Our goals

Understanding and Respect

Promote intercultural understanding, respect and trust through dialogue and education.

Meaningful Dialogue

Encourage ongoing conversations between religious communities and people from different backgrounds.

Inclusion and Acceptance

Develop greater understanding and acceptance of religious, cultural and racial minorities.

Education and Learning

Educate the wider community about the beliefs and practices of world religions through school programs and guest speakers.

OUR JOURNEY

Our history

2003 – The beginning of Project Abraham

The Jewish Community Council of South Australia and the Adelaide Muslim community joined together to establish Project Abraham, promoting peace and reconciliation between Muslims and Jews.

2005 – A growing partnership

The South Australian Council of Churches joined Project Abraham, expanding the initiative to include another Abrahamic faith community.

2011 – Establishment of the Institute

The Jewish Community Council of South Australia and Beit Shalom Synagogue established The Abraham Institute in Adelaide, continuing the original ideals of Project Abraham.

2016 – The Golden Rule Exhibition

The Golden Rule Exhibition brought together artwork and perspectives from diverse spiritual communities, celebrating our shared humanity.

2018 onwards – Expanding our impact

Intercultural understanding became a key objective. In partnership with Together For Humanity, the Institute expanded its work in schools, supporting students and educators to build more inclusive communities.
