STRONGER TOGETHER

Our Partners

Our partners help us to expand our reach and fulfil our mission. Working in close collaboration, we help more students, teachers and parents to develop the skills and confidence to interact with people from different cultural and faith backgrounds.

Through these relationships, we work towards building positive, respectful relationships and creating communities where people feel understood, valued and included.

OUR MENTOR ORGANISATION

Together For Humanity

Together For Humanity is more than a partner; it is our mentor organisation.

Its support and guidance have helped the Abraham Institute develop its work and strengthen its capacity to support students, teachers and communities in developing intercultural understanding.

This relationship reflects the importance of collaboration in creating opportunities for people from different cultural and faith backgrounds to connect, learn from one another and build respectful relationships.

Acknowledgement of Support

Funding for The Abraham Institute during 2022–2025 was provided by Together For Humanity through the Australian Government Department of Education.
