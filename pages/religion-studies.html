Bringing Religion Studies to Life

The Abraham Institute offers bespoke workshops for schools, providing students across all year levels with opportunities to deepen their understanding of religion and spirituality.

Our educators draw on their knowledge and experience of Islam, Christianity and Judaism to help students explore religious beliefs, traditions and expressions, while recognising both the similarities and differences between these faith traditions.

Through engaging discussions and learning experiences, students are encouraged to ask questions, consider different perspectives and develop a deeper understanding of the role religion plays in society.

Exploring Three Faith Traditions

Learning about the traditions, beliefs and practices of three major world religions.

Islam

Exploring Islamic beliefs, prayer, sacred texts, traditions and the ways Islam is expressed in everyday life.

Christianity

Understanding Christian beliefs, scripture, worship, religious symbols and the diversity of Christian traditions.

Judaism

Learning about Jewish beliefs, sacred texts, rituals, festivals and the significance of tradition and community.

What Students Can Explore

Meditation and prayer, including their significance and expression across different faith traditions.

Interpretations of sacred texts and the ways religious teachings are understood.

Images and understandings of God, spirituality and religious belief.

Religious symbols, expressions, rituals and festivals.

How Islam, Christianity and Judaism find expression in society.

Similarities and differences in religious beliefs and practices, including guidance for social and moral beliefs.

Tensions between religious or spiritual beliefs and contemporary social practices.

How religious beliefs and practices have been maintained, adapted and changed over time.

Religious perspectives on ethics, social justice and contemporary issues.

Developing Critical Understanding

Supporting students to think critically, ask questions and form informed perspectives.

Open Discussion

Exploring contemporary issues through respectful discussion and consideration of different religious perspectives.

Analysis and Evaluation

Learning how to identify, analyse and evaluate religious perspectives on contemporary issues.

Ethics and Social Justice

Considering Islamic, Christian and Jewish perspectives on ethical questions and social justice issues.

Personal Perspectives

Encouraging students to explore, evaluate and justify their own perspectives on particular issues.

Support for Student Research Projects

Abraham Institute educators are also available to assist students undertaking research projects on religious questions. Students can benefit from the knowledge and experience of educators with expertise in different faith traditions, helping them investigate questions and develop a more informed understanding of their chosen topic.

The Golden Rule

The Golden Rule — treating others as we would like to be treated — is a value shared across many religious and spiritual traditions. It provides a powerful starting point for exploring common values, mutual respect and our shared humanity.

Explore our Golden Rule resources to discover stories, perspectives and learning materials from different faith communities.
