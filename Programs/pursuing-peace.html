ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Creating understanding through connection

The Abraham Institute's School Presentations, which are based on the Together For Humanity model, aim to assist with the development of children's Intercultural Understanding in South Australian schools.

Hatred and intolerance, or acceptance and respect, are learnt early. Prejudice and hatred dividing people from different cultural and religious backgrounds are a threat to a harmonious society in Australia.

The Abraham Institute works in South Australian schools to help young people learn to coexist peacefully through intercultural understanding.

SHARING PERSPECTIVES

Many ways to be Australian

Through interaction and question-and-answer activities, students explore the many ways people can be Australian and how shared values bring us together.

Students learn about the importance of feeling included and being part of society. They have the opportunity to meet educators from diverse religious and cultural backgrounds, perhaps for the first time, and ask questions in a welcoming and safe environment.

INTERFAITH FRAMEWORK

Exploring religious and cultural perspectives

Available for independent schools or by request, the interfaith framework can explore:

  • Belief in one God, many Gods or no God
  • How we pray and different houses of worship
  • Dietary laws and traditions
  • Holy books, important stories and legends
